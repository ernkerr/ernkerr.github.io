Movie List — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 August 2026

Movie List does not collect your data. There are no accounts, no analytics, and no advertising. Everything you save stays on your phone.

What we collect

Nothing. Movie List has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising SDKs, and no server of our own. We cannot see your lists, and we do not know who you are.

What stays on your device

Everything you create in the app is stored only on your phone:

  • the movies and shows you save, and whether you have watched them
  • your star ratings
  • who recommended each title, and any note about why
  • your name, if you enter one in Settings

This data is removed when you delete the app, and “Reset app” in Settings erases it immediately. If you use iCloud device backups, Apple may include this data in your encrypted backup; that copy is governed by Apple’s privacy policy, not ours.

Services the app contacts

To show movie information, the app requests data from:

  • TMDB (themoviedb.org) — titles, posters, cast, synopses, streaming availability
  • OMDb (omdbapi.com) — IMDb, Rotten Tomatoes and Metacritic scores

These requests contain only the title or identifier being looked up. No name, email, device identifier, or list contents are ever sent. Each service receives your device’s IP address as an unavoidable part of any internet request, and handles it under its own privacy policy.

Sharing a recommendation

When you send a recommendation, the app hands the message to whichever app you choose — Messages, Mail, or another. It contains the title, and the note and name you chose to include. It goes only to the person you send it to. We never see it.

Subscriptions

If you subscribe, the purchase is handled entirely by Apple. We receive no payment details and never see your card. Apple tells the app only whether an active subscription exists.

Children

Movie List is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
