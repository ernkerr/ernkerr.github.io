Farkle Score Tracker — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026

Farkle Score Tracker does not collect your data. There are no accounts, no analytics, and no advertising. Your games stay on your phone.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no sign-up, no analytics or tracking SDKs, no advertising, and no server of our own. We cannot see your games, and we do not know who you are.

What stays on your device

Everything you create in the app is stored only on your phone:

  • player names
  • rounds, turns, and scores
  • the house rules you choose for each game

This data is removed when you delete the app. If you use iCloud device backups, Apple may include it in your encrypted backup; that copy is governed by Apple’s privacy policy, not ours.

Services the app contacts

  • Apple App Store — when you subscribe or restore a purchase, and to check whether a subscription is active.
  • Expo (expo.dev) — when the app opens, it may check for a bug-fix update to the app. The request identifies the app version, not you.

No names, scores, or game data are ever sent. Each service receives your device’s IP address as an unavoidable part of any internet request, and handles it under its own privacy policy.

Subscriptions

If you subscribe, the purchase is handled entirely by Apple. We receive no payment details and never see your card. Apple tells the app only whether an active subscription exists.

Children

Farkle Score Tracker is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
